# Push/Pull

Push/Pull gives a flat face some thickness. It is the step that turns your drawings into solid parts, and it is one of the most satisfying tools to use.

## Pushing a face

Press `P` for the Push/Pull tool. Click a face, then move the cursor along the direction you want to pull it out or push it in. A preview shows the new shape as you move, so you can see the result before you commit. Click again to finish. If you move the cursor over a corner elsewhere in the model, the height snaps to match that corner, which is a simple way to keep two parts level. A distance of zero does nothing, `Esc` abandons the push, and undo takes back a finished one. When you are learning, use the preview freely: nothing changes until the second click.

## Typing a distance

While you are pushing, type a length and press `Enter` to set the distance exactly. A negative number reverses the direction, so you can push inward instead of pulling outward. Right after a push, you can type a new length and press `Enter` to change that push instead of adding another one. This is useful when you finish a pull and immediately decide it should be a little thicker or thinner; no extra step is needed.

## Repeating a push

Double click another face to push it by the same distance as the last push. This saves time when several faces need the same thickness, such as the walls of a tray or the ribs of a bracket. Check the preview, then let the double click do the work. Once you are comfortable with this, you can build up surprisingly detailed parts from a few flat shapes and a series of consistent pushes.
