# Materials and painting

Materials give faces a color or a texture so the model is easier to read and so the design is easier to plan. The panels and buttons below work on faces, groups and components alike.

## Paint Bucket

The Paint Bucket tool has key `B` and the toolbar label Paint Bucket. Click a face or a group or component to paint it with the current material. If what you clicked is part of the selection, the whole selection is painted. The side of the face you see is the side that gets painted. Painting with the default material takes paint off. Painting with what is already there does nothing.

## The Materials panel

View > Materials opens the Materials panel. It shows a list with a color swatch for each material. Click one to make it the Paint Bucket's current material. New... and Edit... open a form with a name, color, opacity, an image file for a texture, and the image's real width and height in the model's unit. Purge removes the materials that nothing uses.

## Textures

View > Texture Position opens a panel to move, turn and scale the texture on the selected face. It has fields such as Move X, Move Y, Turn in degrees, Scale X and Scale Y. The texture stays on that face while you work with the rest of the model.

## Picking a material from the model

Hold `Alt` and click with the Paint Bucket to take the material from a face or group. You can also use the eyedropper button in the Materials panel and click anything in the model; that also switches to the Paint Bucket so you can keep painting.
